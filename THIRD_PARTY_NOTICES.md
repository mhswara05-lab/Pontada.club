# Open-source attribution

Pontada Pickleball adapts the circle rotation construction and standings accumulation from [rbongard/tournament-scheduler](https://github.com/rbongard/tournament-scheduler), copyright (c) 2026 Richard Bongard, MIT licensed. The unmodified upstream modules and license are retained in `lib/upstream/`.

Pontada adds capacity-preserving schedule allocation, strict partnership validation, total-points rules, persistent optimistic concurrency, operation deduplication, a new Indonesian interface, and Cloudflare D1 storage. This is a focused adaptation of the domain engines, not a full deployment of the upstream club-management application.
